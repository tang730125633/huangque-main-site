# Frame Direction

## Canvas
- 1080×1920 portrait
- preserve full-bleed talking-head footage
- reserve top 360px for hook/headline overlays
- reserve bottom 330px for captions, progress and metadata

## Palette
- ink: #05070b
- paper: #ffffff
- Huangque yellow: #f4ca3b
- glass: rgba(3,5,9,.86)

## Typography
- Noto Sans SC 900 for hook, captions and semantic headlines
- JetBrains Mono 600 for technical labels and English microcopy

## Motion rules
- spring-pop entrance for hook and CTA
- kinetic beat slam for semantic headline words
- waterfall sequencing for caption cards
- subtle punch zoom around semantic transitions

## Safety
- do not cover the primary face with captions
- captions remain above the bottom platform UI zone
- no CSS animation; all choreography lives in one paused GSAP timeline
