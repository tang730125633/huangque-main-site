Moon texture attribution
========================

Source: Solar System Scope, "8K Moon Texture"
Source page: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
Archive page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Solarsystemscope_texture_8k_moon.jpg
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The source is an equirectangular lunar surface map based on NASA elevation and
imagery data. For this site it was resized from 8192x4096 to 4096x2048,
lightly sharpened after downsampling, and encoded as WebP.
